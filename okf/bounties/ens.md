# What ENSv2 offers

Hierarchical registry structure; wildcard resolution off a parent's resolver; deploying your own subname
registry to tokenize and manage subnames under your own rules; Enhanced Access Control as the shared
role-based permission system behind registries and resolvers; Permissioned Resolvers giving subnames
ownership of their own data; record aliasing at the resolver level; namespace aliasing via a shared registry.
Subname setups can be expiring, revocable, non-transferable or transferable, including forever names with no
parent control.

Bonus points are offered for bringing AI agents into the mix — agents as namespaces, each with their own
identity and permissions.
